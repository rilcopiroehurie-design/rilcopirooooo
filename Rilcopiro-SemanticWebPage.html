Introduction

The Philippines has more than 7,000 islands, and each region keeps its own history and scenery. This page visits five places worth seeing: two Spanish-era towns, a lagoon inside a cave, a lighthouse on a green hilltop, and a church famous for its carved facade.

Intramuros,Manila

Intramuros is the historic walled city of Manila, built by the Spanish in the late 16th century as the seat of colonial government. Its cobblestone streets, centuries-old churches like San Agustin Church, and fortress walls like Fort Santiago make it a living window into the Philippines' colonial past.

Region
Luzon
Known for
Fort Santiago, San Agustin Church

Vigan,Ilocos Sur

Vigan, the capital of Ilocos Sur, is one of the best-preserved Spanish colonial towns in Asia and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its cobblestone streets, such as the famous Calle Crisologo, are lined with centuries-old ancestral houses and traveled by horse-drawn kalesas, offering a vivid glimpse into the country's colonial heritage.

Region
Luzon
Known for
Calle Crisologo, kalesa rides

Sohoton Cove, Samar

A hidden cave lagoon with glowing turquoise water, framed by a ceiling of hanging stalactites. Sunlight filters through the cave mouth, lighting up the lush green cliffs outside and the crystal-clear water below.

Region
Visayas
Known for
Cave lagoon, turquoise water

Tayid Lighthouse, Batanes

A white lighthouse with a red-capped lantern and a red-roofed keeper's house sits on a rolling green hilltop above the deep blue Pacific. Grassy slopes, rugged coastal cliffs, and a cloud-crowned island in the distance make this one of the most scenic viewpoints in Batanes.

Region
Luzon (northernmost islands)
Known for
Ocean views, rolling hills

Miagao Church, Iloilo

A UNESCO-listed Baroque landmark built of golden adobe and coral stone, with two sturdy, asymmetrical bell towers and a richly carved facade. Its centerpiece is a coconut tree relief above a statue of St. Christopher, set against a bright blue sky and framed by a colorful garden.

Region
Visayas
Known for
Carved facade, Baroque design
